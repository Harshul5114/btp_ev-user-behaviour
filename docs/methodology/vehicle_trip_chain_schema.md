# Interim NHTS household-vehicle trip chains

Generated by `notebooks/03_trip_chains.ipynb` from the unmodified 2022 NHTS public Trip and Vehicle files. Both CSVs are **interim, candidate mobility data**, not verified complete vehicle trajectories or final model inputs. They are ignored by Git and can be regenerated by running the notebook. Source-field meanings and codes are documented in [nhts_variable_mapping.md](nhts_variable_mapping.md) and the official NHTS PDFs under `data/raw/nhts_2022/docs/`.

## Population and chronology

The filter is identical to notebook 02: household vehicle (`TRPHHVEH=01`), driver (`DRVR_FLG=01`), rostered `VEHCASEID`, privately operated `TRPTRANS` mode 01/02/03/04/06/07, valid HHMM clocks, nonnegative trip minutes/miles, and clock duration consistent with `TRVLCMIN`. No person-trip rows are deduplicated. Entire vehicle-days with simultaneous same-vehicle starts or overlapping driver intervals are held out. The result is 19,673 candidate legs from 6,874 vehicles. The nine invalid measurement rows and 69 conflicted vehicle-days are diagnosed in the notebook; they are not exported into these two CSVs.

NHTS travel days run 04:00–04:00. `start_min` and `end_min` are local clock minutes after midnight (0–1439). `survey_start_min` adds 1,440 to starts before 04:00, and `survey_end_min = survey_start_min + trip_duration_min`, allowing midnight crossings. Values beyond 1,680 mean a trip extends past the nominal survey-day endpoint. These are **relative minutes**, not absolute dates. Public `TDAYDATE` is YYYYMM only.

## `data/interim/vehicle_trip_chains.csv`

Grain: **one retained candidate driver person-trip**, ordered within `VEHCASEID` by `vehicle_leg_index`. `TDCASEID` is the source person-trip ID, not a unique physical vehicle-movement ID.

| Fields | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `HOUSEID`, `VEHCASEID`, `VEHID`, `PERSONID`, `TDCASEID`, `SEQ_TRIPID` | Original household, vehicle, person, and person-trip identifiers; `SEQ_TRIPID` is person-level, while `vehicle_leg_index` is the derived vehicle ordering. |
| `TRAVDAY`, `TDAYDATE` | Original reported day of week and travel month (YYYYMM). No exact day is inferred. |
| `STRTTIME`, `ENDTIME`, `start_min`, `end_min`, `survey_start_min`, `survey_end_min`, `crosses_midnight` | Original HHMM clocks and derived time coordinates/flag. |
| `trip_duration_min`, `trip_distance_miles` | Numeric versions of documented `TRVLCMIN` and `TRPMILES`. |
| `WHYFROM`, `WHYTO`, `origin_group`, `destination_group` | Original purpose codes and derived presentation groups. Home = 01/02; Work = 03; Shopping/errands = 13/14; Social/recreation = 15–18; Other = remaining documented categories (including work-related other 04/05). Unknown codes remain Unknown. Purpose is an activity report, not coordinates. |
| `TRPTRANS`, `travel_mode_label` | Original documented travel mode and readable label. |
| `WTTRDFIN`, `DWELTIME` | Original seven-day **trip** weight and original person time at destination. The trip weight must not simply be reused as a vehicle-day or parking-interval weight. Negative dwell codes retain their source values. |
| `continuity_to_next` | `match`, `mismatch`, `unknown`, or `no_next` for current `WHYTO` versus next vehicle leg's `WHYFROM`. |
| `chain_status` | `complete` when all internal purpose transitions match; `mismatch` when at least one differs; `unknown` when there is no mismatch but at least one unknown code; `single_leg` when continuity is untestable. |
| `begins_home`, `ends_home` | Whether the first observed origin / final observed destination has a Home code. These do not imply a complete day or overnight parking. |

## `data/interim/vehicle_parking_intervals.csv`

Grain: **one gap between consecutive retained candidate legs of the same vehicle** (12,799 rows). `TDCASEID` identifies the arriving leg and `next_TDCASEID` the following departing leg. No interval before the first leg or after the last leg is fabricated.

| Fields | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `HOUSEID`, `VEHCASEID`, `TDCASEID`, `next_TDCASEID`, `PERSONID`, `next_PERSONID` | Source IDs for both bordering person trips. |
| `WHYTO`, `next_WHYFROM`, `destination_group` | Destination of arriving leg, origin of next leg, and grouped destination. |
| `parking_start_min`, `parking_end_min`, `parking_start_clock`, `parking_end_clock`, `parking_duration_min` | Arrival-to-next-departure gap; numeric times use the 04:00–04:00 survey coordinate, clock strings are HH:MM and may wrap midnight. The name “parking” is shorthand for an **inferred between-trip gap**, not measured stationary presence. |
| `continuity_to_next`, `chain_status`, `same_driver` | Purpose and respondent continuity diagnostics; no mismatch is repaired. |
| `within_survey_window`, `analysis_eligible` | Interval inside 04:00–04:00; eligible iff the *entire* multi-leg chain has complete purpose continuity and the interval is inside that window. Non-eligible rows are preserved for audit and should not enter the notebook's parking statistics. |
| `DWELTIME`, `dwell_valid_min`, `gap_minus_dwell_min` | Original person dwell, nonnegative numeric dwell where recorded, and derived gap minus that dwell for comparison. Missing numeric values are blank in the CSV. |

The main parking figures and summary use only `analysis_eligible=true` intervals (12,159 in the current data), as **unweighted sample descriptions**. This includes 600 zero-minute gaps, which represent no positive stationary opportunity but are retained rather than silently discarded. The source Trip weight supports trip/mileage estimates under the User Guide, not a ready-made national estimate for constructed vehicle gaps. Even an eligible purpose match is not geographic proof that the vehicle stayed parked throughout, and the final destination has no observed overnight duration.
